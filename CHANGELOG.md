## 0.0.1

* **Initial Release:** Official launch of `horizontal_credit_card`.
* **Native Landscape Orientation:** Engineered specifically for horizontal credit and debit cards conforming to the ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 aspect ratio ($1.586 : 1$).
* **Hardware-Accelerated 3D Physics:** Real-time gyroscopic perspective tilt with dynamic specular reflection and 180° flip animation.
* **Sensitive Data Protection:** Tap-to-reveal privacy masking for 16-digit card numbers and CVV.
* **Special Financial States:** Built-in support for `isFrozen` (frosted crystal overlay with security padlock) and `isExpired` states.
* **Zero External Dependencies:** Built purely on Flutter core SDK for 100% enterprise compatibility and 120 FPS performance.
